Delete your account

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time. Choose the option that works best for you.

Option 1 — In-app (instant)

Open LiftOff → Settings → scroll to the bottom → tap Delete account. Your workout history, body logs, and profile are deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Email request

Send us an email from the address linked to your account and we will delete it within 7 days.

Send email request

Data deleted includes: workout sessions, set logs, body weight entries, and your profile. Your auth account will be fully removed within 7 days of the request.
